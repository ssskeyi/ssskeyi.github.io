Research Interest

My research centers on Embodied AI, investigating how artificial agents can develop generalizable, task-aware policies that operate robustly in cluttered real-world 3D scenes. I am particularly interested in the synergy between 3D scene understanding and imitation learning, striving to build policies that can effectively attend to task-relevant regions while maintaining generalization across diverse environments.

ECCV 2026

Attention-DP3: Spatially Object-aware 3D Diffusion Policy via Geometry-aligned Attentional Conditioning

While DP3 demonstrates strong 3D spatial awareness and modeling capabilities, its original architecture pays insufficient attention to task-relevant regions, limiting its generalization. We introduce a novel attention mechanism that lifts 2D masks into 3D space and guides the model to focus more on target regions through attention fields across three dimensions. This enables a compact policy to maintain robust performance even as scene complexity increases.
